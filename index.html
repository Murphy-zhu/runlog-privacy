跑步记录 · 隐私政策

更新日期:2026-08-24  |  联系邮箱:zhuyuqiu@qq.com
一句话承诺

「跑步记录」没有服务器。你的所有跑步数据只存在你自己的 iPhone / Apple Watch 里,我们不上传、不收集、不出售任何个人数据。

1. 我们收集的信息

  • 位置信息:当你开始跑步时,App 会请求定位权限,用于记录 GPS 跑步轨迹与配速。轨迹数据仅保存在设备本地。
  • 相机(可选):使用「拍照记录」功能时,照片仅保存在本机并标注到你的跑步路线,不会上传。
  • 健康数据(如后续接入):仅在获得你明确授权后读取 Apple 健康中的运动数据(如心率),用于展示跑步指标,不会上传。

2. 信息的使用与存储

  • 所有跑步数据默认只保存在你的设备本地,App 没有任何服务器。
  • 卸载 App 即删除全部本地数据。

3. 第三方服务

本 App 当前不使用任何第三方分析或广告 SDK。

4. 儿童隐私

本 App 不针对 13 岁以下儿童设计,也不故意收集儿童信息。

RunLog · Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-24 | Contact: zhuyuqiu@qq.com
One-line promise

RunLog has no server. All your running data stays on your own iPhone / Apple Watch. We do not upload, collect, or sell any personal data.

1. Information We Collect

  • Location: When you start a run, we request location access to record your GPS route and pace. Route data stays on-device only.
  • Camera (optional): Photos taken via the photo feature are stored locally and pinned to your route. Never uploaded.
  • Health data (if integrated later): Read only with your explicit permission, for metrics like heart rate. Never uploaded.

2. Use & Storage

  • All run data is stored locally on your device. RunLog has no servers.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all local data.

3. Third-Party Services

RunLog does not use any third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

4. Children's Privacy

RunLog is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

Questions? Email us at zhuyuqiu@qq.com

6. Policy Updates

We may update this policy from time to time; changes will be posted on this page.
